Omo · Skyline floor

Live shells on every desk. Web pages on the walls. Everything stays where you left it.

Move

W A S D walk · Shift sprint · Space jump · C crouch · mouse to look

Use a screen

Left-click a monitor or wall when you are close. Right-click any screen you can see, from anywhere in the room, to control it without walking over. Esc to go back to walking.

Persistence

Each desk is its own tmux session. Reload, walk away, come back: the shell, its jobs and its scrollback are still there. Wall addresses are remembered per wall.
