# Assets (baked-render lane)

- herringbone_parquet (diff/rough/nor_gl 2k) https://polyhaven.com/a/herringbone_parquet
- wood_table_001 (diff/rough 1k) https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001
- dark_wood (diff 1k) https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_wood
- marble_01 (diff/rough 1k) https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
