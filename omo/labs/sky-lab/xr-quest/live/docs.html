Omo · Skyline floor

Live shells on every desk. Web pages on the walls. Everything stays where you left it.

Move

W A S D walk · Shift sprint · Space jump · C crouch · mouse to look

Screens

+ Terminal and + Web page (top left) put a new screen in front of you. Drag the bar above a screen to move it; let go near a wall and it lies flat on the wall. Drag the corner to resize. The x closes it.

Use a screen

Left-click a screen when you are close. Right-click any screen you can see, from anywhere in the room, to control it without walking over. Esc to go back to walking.

Persistence

Each terminal screen is its own tmux session. Reload, walk away, come back: the shell, its jobs and its scrollback are still there, and every screen is where you left it.
